## Description

A census of the base stations in one 1.25 MHz channel of Spreading Rate 1. Each capture of 0.98 s is searched for the pilot PN at every code phase, coherently over segments of 1024 chips and in carrier steps of 506.25 Hz. Each pilot is tracked through the capture (its PN offset, Ec/Io, carrier offset and the sample clock error), and its Sync Channel is despread with Walsh 32, deinterleaved, decoded and reassembled into Sync Channel Messages. A pilot without a message is shown by its offset from the strongest pilot, and by a derived PN offset when another pilot of the capture has a message. The Paging Channel and everything after it are not decoded.

Standard: 3GPP2 C.S0002-C (physical layer), C.S0004 (signalling capsule, CRC-30), C.S0005 (Sync Channel Message).

The test signal uses the 48-tap baseband filter of C.S0002-C 3.1.3.1.17.1. The matched filter of the receiver at two samples a chip is derived from it (adjacent taps added); the standard gives the filter only for the transmitter. The band class formulas (classes 0, 1 and 4) are worldwide channel arithmetic of the standard; which band class a region uses is region data, and the host fills `band_class` from it.
