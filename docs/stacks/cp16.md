# CP16

16 carriers keyed by 16 x 16 dot characters: a picture of the dots and the characters read against a font. Receive and transmit.

Stack id: `cp16`. Group: HF and utility.

## Description

CP16: 16 carriers 5 to 30 Hz apart, each keyed on and off by one column of 16 × 16 dot characters, one dot row per time step, top row first, then a blank row (17 rows a character, 1 to 4 s). The receiver takes both sidebands as 8 kHz complex audio, finds the comb of carriers and the row clock every second over the last 20 s, draws the rows as a picture of 16 dots, and reads the characters against the GB 2312 glyphs of a font (HZK16 or TrueType, named by the `font` setting) or, without one, the shared Latin, Cyrillic and Greek glyphs. The stack transmits: `send` sends a text with the four transmit parameters; the host's transmit switch must be on. Not confirmed by a public document: the lowest carrier (1000 Hz here), the font, the blank cell for a space, the full-width forms of ASCII and the raised edges of the keying. T/RAC 019-2019 was not read. Rows shorter than about 1.3 over the spacing (1 s at 20 Hz, 1.5 s at 12 Hz) blur their edges and the row clock fails.

## Tuning: notes

The stack holds no calling frequency: the usual dials are in `crates/stacks/sdrkit-stack-hfkbd/data/slow_dials.csv`, for the host to offer.

## Settings: notes

Advanced settings. The owner works without any of them set.
