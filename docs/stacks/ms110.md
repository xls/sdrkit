## Description

2400 Bd 8-PSK on 1800 Hz, 75 to 4800 bit/s: the rate and interleaver from the preamble, an equaliser, the K = 7 code; text and STANAG 5066 D_PDUs. The receiver follows MIL-STD-188-110C section 5.3.2: it finds a preamble segment by its known head, reads D1, D2 and the segment count, trains a decision-feedback equaliser (33 taps at half a symbol, 12 feedback taps) on the preamble and on the known symbols of each cycle, and decodes each interleaver block with the K = 7 Viterbi decoder until the end-of-message pattern 4B65A5B2. The bytes are shown least significant bit first, and STANAG 5066 D_PDUs found in the bits fill a table. Not confirmed by a public document: the D_PDU layout and its CRCs, and the matched filter. The 0.0 s interleaver and the 2400 bit/s voice setting are not decoded; the acquisition range is +-130 Hz.
