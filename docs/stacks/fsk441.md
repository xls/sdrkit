## Description

Meteor scatter pings: 4-FSK at 441 baud, three tones a character (882, 1323, 1764 and 2205 Hz). A burst finder on the audio, taken before the slow AGC of the front end, hands each burst to a reader that finds the tone offset, the symbol phase and the character boundary. There is no slot clock. Not confirmed by a public document: the rows of the character table, that codes 0, 21 and 42 read as a space, and the two spaces the test signal sends between repeats.

## Tuning: notes

The stack holds no calling frequency: the usual dials are in `crates/stacks/sdrkit-stack-wsjt/data/scatter_dials.csv`, for the host to offer.

## Settings: notes

Advanced settings. The owner works without any of them set.
