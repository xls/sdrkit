## Description

Naval broadcast FSK: one +-425 Hz channel or up to 16 sub-carriers of +-42.5 Hz; the speed of each, plain ITA2 text, encrypted channels measured. The receiver filters the mark and space tones of the 16 sub-carriers at 255 + 170 n Hz (filters of 1/85 s, so the other tones of the 85 Hz grid fall on their nulls) and of a single channel found by a spectrum search, measures the noise between and outside the sub-carriers, decides each bit with a hysteresis, finds the speed from the run lengths, and runs four start-stop readers a channel (45.45, 50, 75 and 100 Bd); the best is read. A channel with no reader that fits is reported as synchronous (encrypted) by its speed only. STANAG 4481 is sold, so nothing here is confirmed by a public document.
