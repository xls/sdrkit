# STANAG 4539 high-rate serial tone

3200 to 12800 bit/s on 1800 Hz (QPSK to 64-QAM): rate and interleaver from the preamble, probe-based MMSE equaliser, tail-biting code; text and STANAG 5066 D_PDUs.

Stack id: `s4539`. Group: HF utility.

STANAG 4539 high-rate serial tone (the waveform of MIL-STD-188-110C Appendix C): 3200 to 12800 bit/s in QPSK, 8-PSK and 16-, 32- and 64-QAM; a 287-symbol preamble, frames of 256 symbols with 31-symbol mini-probes, the 103-symbol block every 72 frames. ```text iq -> [hf_front 24 kHz] -> [s4539: matched filter, block search, probe channel estimates, MMSE DFE, deinterleaver, tail-biting Viterbi] -> text, D_PDUs, events ```

Standard: MIL-STD-188-110C with Change 1, Appendix C.

## Description

3200 to 12800 bit/s on 1800 Hz (QPSK to 64-QAM): rate and interleaver from the preamble, probe-based MMSE equaliser, tail-biting code; text and STANAG 5066 D_PDUs. The receiver follows MIL-STD-188-110C Appendix C: it finds the 103-symbol block by the products of neighbouring known symbols, takes a coarse frequency from the products of known symbols 4 apart and the fine one from the 16-symbol period of the probes and the D values from the best fit of the valid combinations, estimates a 16-tap channel from every 31-symbol probe, designs an MMSE decision-feedback equaliser every 16 symbols from the channel interpolated between two probes, and decodes each interleaver block with the tail-biting Viterbi decoder. It starts at the preamble or joins at a reinserted block, and follows a rate change at a reinserted block. Not confirmed by a public document: the D_PDU layout and its CRCs; the scrambler follows Figure C-5 as drawn. Acquisition from the mini-probes alone is not done; the acquisition range is +-96 Hz.
